### Installing on Android
1. Open the downloaded **nightjar-radio.apk**.
2. If asked, allow your browser to **install unknown apps**, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap **More details → Install anyway**. This happens with any app that isn't from the Play Store.
4. Open Nightjar Radio. Future updates pop up inside the app.

### What's in it
- Six presets, a real tuning dial and a spinning globe
- Song titles, when the station shares them
- Wake-up radio alarm and bedtime mode
- Home-screen widgets and Android Auto
