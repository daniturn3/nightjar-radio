Thank you

Nightjar Radio is built on the generosity of others:

  • radio-browser.info — the free, community-run directory of radio stations behind every search, list and “What’s on”.
  • The broadcasters — every station you hear belongs to, and is streamed by, its own broadcaster.
  • ESPN and TheSportsDB — fixtures and live scores on the Sport tab.

It isn’t a company, and it isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any radio station or broadcaster, ESPN, TheSportsDB, radio-browser.info, Rockstar Games, the IOC or IPC, FIFA, UEFA, World Rugby, the ICC or any other sports body. Names of stations, teams and tournaments are used only to describe what’s on.
