Nightjar Radio

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Nightjar Radio doesn’t collect analytics or sell anything about you.
  • Your stuff stays on your phone. Favourites, presets, settings, your birthday and listening history are stored only on your device.
  • I can’t see who you are or what you listen to. Nightjar Radio has no server of its own that receives your data.
  • To work, the app talks to a few outside services (listed below) — mainly the station directory and the radio stations themselves.

Who runs Nightjar Radio

Nightjar Radio is a free hobby project made in the UK by an independent developer. It isn’t a company, and it isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any radio station or broadcaster, ESPN, TheSportsDB, radio-browser.info, Rockstar Games, the IOC or IPC, FIFA, UEFA, World Rugby, the ICC or any other sports body. Names of stations, teams and tournaments are used only to describe what’s on. You can contact me on Reddit at u/Daniturn1 or on Telegram at @daniturn3.

What’s stored on your device

Everything you set up in the app is saved only on your phone (or in your browser, if you use the web version):

  • Favourites, presets, recently played stations and your Home layout
  • Settings, such as home country, themes, sleep timer, data saver and alarm
  • Your birthday, if you add one (day and month only — used just to show a birthday surprise)
  • A listening log used for your weekly recap, Radio Wrapped and the data-use estimate

In the Android app, a small backup copy of a few of these (birthday, presets, favourites, layout and some settings) is also kept in the app’s own private storage, so they survive your phone clearing its cache. None of this is sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. You can also save your own backup file in Settings → Back up now.

Android permissions

  • Internet and network status — to stream radio, reconnect if your signal drops, and offer Wi-Fi-only options.
  • Location (only if you tap “Find stations near me”) — rounded to about 1 km and used only to find local stations.
  • Notifications and playing in the background — for the playback controls and keeping radio on with the screen off.
  • Alarms and starting after a restart — for the wake-up radio alarm.
  • Battery optimisation (only if you allow it) — so the alarm and background play aren’t stopped by your phone.

Problem reports and ideas

If you send a report or idea from the app, it’s stored in a private Google spreadsheet that only I can see, and a short copy is sent to my phone through ntfy.sh. I use reports only to fix problems and improve the app — never for marketing, and they’re never sold or shared.

  • What’s included: exactly what’s shown on the report screen before you tap Send. Adding a name or username is optional.
  • Why: you choose to send it, and I have a legitimate interest in fixing the app you’re using.
  • How long it’s kept: reports are deleted within 12 months of being sent.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete a report you sent — message me on Reddit (u/Daniturn1) or Telegram (@daniturn3) with roughly when you sent it and what it said. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Nightjar Radio isn’t aimed at children. Apart from problem reports you choose to send, it doesn’t collect personal information at all — please don’t include personal details in a report.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without location — just don’t tap “Find stations near me”.
  • You can turn off song titles in lists, seasonal themes and sound effects in Settings.
  • To delete everything, clear the app’s data in your phone’s settings or uninstall it.
